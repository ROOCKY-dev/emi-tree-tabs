## EMI Tree Tabs 6.0.0 for Minecraft 1.20.1

Supports Fabric and Forge.

- Tabs, sidebar controls and popups now use EMI’s textures and follow your resource pack.
- Drag the sidebar edge to resize it, or unpin it to tuck it away. Turn off the sidebar frame to leave just the items and groups visible.
- Expanded groups give nested tabs more room. The toolbar and tab strip also have more space around their controls.
- Fixed items showing through the tab strip, the missing EMI help tooltip and the Fabric crafting sidebar configuration being cleared on startup.

The sidebar geometry was checked offline. In-game verification is pending.
